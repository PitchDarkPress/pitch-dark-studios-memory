## FUTURE PLATFORMS

### Online Newspapers and Magazines Platform
A separate platform for creating and publishing online newspapers
and magazines. Different product — multiple contributors, editorial
workflow, layout and design, regular publication schedules, reader
subscription management. Same Pitch Dark Studios family, different
tool. Write in InkySwot, publish to the magazine platform.
InkySwot is the creation end. The magazine platform is the
broadcast end.
Decision: logged. Do not build yet.

### Rights Marketplace
Marketplace where writers sell articles, short stories, interviews,
columns, reviews to publishers. Two user types: Writers and
Publishers. Write it in InkySwot, sell it in the marketplace.
Fills a genuine gap — no clean accessible rights marketplace
exists for independent writers.
Decision: logged. Not on current build list.

## NICE TO HAVE — APP FEATURES

### Collaboration Mode
Multiple human users on the same project simultaneously.
Real-time sync, conflict resolution.
Requires Supabase and careful design. Post-launch.

### Version History and Snapshots
Save project snapshots at key moments.
Roll back to any previous version. Post-launch.

### AI Voice Profiles
Named AI voice profiles combining style, register, vocabulary
and pacing. User builds and saves their own profiles over time.
Beyond the basic Style Field.

### Alpha Poems — AI Reader Content
JSON-format poems written for AI readers, not humans.
Two completed. Could become a feature or product.
Logged for future consideration.

### Shakespeare In English
Translation series — Shakespeare in plain modern English.
Credited to Marlowe AI. Already in PDP catalogue.
Could become InkySwot-native or a feature in The Guide.

### AI-Generated Cover Concepts
2 or 3 cover concept images generated on Overview screen
based on project content — title, genre, strap line, outline.
Mood pieces, not finished covers.
Requires image generation API (DALL-E or similar).
Not justified before launch revenue. Revisit post-launch.
