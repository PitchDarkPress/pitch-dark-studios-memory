PLATFORM IDENTITY
InkySwot is a writing platform. Not a content generation engine.
Assisted creation. Not instant generation. Non-negotiable.
Tagline: Publish and be damned. Always.
"Publish and be prepared" must never be used.

INKYSWOT IS THE DCW
The DCW is not a screen, strip, board, or panel. It is the WHOLE
platform. Every section already built (Characters, Locations, Events,
etc.) is part of the instrument. The database is the bones of the story.
Locked: 4 June 2026.

There are THREE views of the same single body of data, and the writer
moves freely between them: THE BOARD (Plot Mapping reborn — the wide
view), THE WP + WHEEL (the close view, one scene), and THE SITE MAP (the
whole-structure view, right sidebar). It is a FUNNEL — wide and loose at

the others. NOTHING ENTERED TWICE. Locked: 9 June 2026.

THE TREATMENT IS THE DCW SWITCHED ON
The Treatment is where everything in the database ASSEMBLES into a
readable, writable whole. A plotline is a VIEW of data that already
exists, not a new thing to author. Locked: 4 June 2026.

THE SPINE IS A TIMELINE OF EVENTS
The Treatment is a timeline of events. Tiers: CHAPTER → SCENE → EVENT.
Only EVENTS sit on the line and become prose — because only events
happen. Characters, Locations, Objects, etc. do not happen; they are
reached into by the events that use them. Locked: 4 June 2026.

FILL IN THE BLANKS
The Treatment assembles the SUBSTANCE from the database (not just
names) and leaves the writer the blanks — the prose and dialogue no
database can hold. Assembled, the sections ARE the draft; the writing is
the last layer, not the first. Locked: 4 June 2026.

THE WP AI PROMPT — PROMPT + EXPAND, SCOPED TO THE SYNOPSIS — LOCKED
(11 June 2026)
The WP carries the writer-facing Ada buttons (+ Prompt / ⟳ Expand),
SCOPED so they cannot write the book.
- THE SCOPE: the button expands the SYNOPSIS of the scene or chapter —
  NOT the prose. Expanding the synopsis thickens the PLAN; the blank the
  writer fills stays blank. "Fill in the blanks" enforced.
- IT SCALES WITH THE SPINE: a CHAPTER + Prompt expands the chapter
  synopsis; a SCENE + Prompt expands that scene's synopsis. (This is why
  the chapter synopsis had to exist.)
- "STOP AI WRITING THE WHOLE THING" IS BUILT INTO THE SHAPE: (a) scoped
  buttons — no "write everything" door; (b) the hidden system prompt is
  OURS and forbids prose, SERVER-SIDE (Steps 5–7); (c) output lands by
  CHOICE, never automatically.
Locked: 11 June 2026.

THE WHEEL — WINDOWED, EASED, STRAIGHT — LOCKED (7 June 2026)
