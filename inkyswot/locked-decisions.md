InkySwot — Locked Decisions
Last updated: 25 May 2026

---

PLATFORM IDENTITY
InkySwot is a writing platform. Not a content generation engine.
Assisted creation. Not instant generation.
Why: content generation engines become sludge infrastructure.
Writing platforms become culturally respected.
This distinction is non-negotiable and must never be compromised.

PRICING
Paid only. No free tier. No exceptions.
Monthly: £9.95. Annual: £99.50 (two months free).
14-day money back guarantee. No questions asked.
Why: free tier is the primary spam abuse vector. Paid only
positions InkySwot as a serious professional tool and
makes mass account farming uneconomical.

PUBLICATION FEE
£1 GBP per publication. Base currency GBP.
Stripe handles international conversion automatically.
Paid users: one free publication per week. £1 after that.
Free users: no longer applicable — no free tier.
Why: anti-scam friction. Not a revenue stream.
Upfront and transparent to all users.
Locked: 24 May 2026.

SECURITY SYSTEM — LOCKED LAYERS
Invisible to genuine users:
- Device fingerprinting
- IP tracking and location detection
- Behaviour pattern detection
- Disposable email detection
- Stripe fraud detection
Visible but acceptable:
- Email verification at signup
- Paid upfront
- 14-day money back guarantee
- Support ticket required for new device
Why: layers of friction that are invisible to real writers
but make spam farming expensive, slow, and detectable.
No single layer stops everything. The layers together do.
Locked: 24 May 2026.

MARKETING POSITIONING — NEVER USE
Never market: "Write books instantly" / "Passive income publishing" /
"Make 100 books a week" / "AI author riches"
Why: these audiences destroy platform quality and attract spam farms.
InkySwot markets to: writers, creators, worldbuilders, playwrights,
serious independents, disabled writers, dyslexic writers.
Locked: 24 May 2026.

Format list locked — Novel / Short Story / Novella / Flash Fiction /
Screenplay / Teleplay / Radio Drama / Audio Drama /
Stage Play / Picture Book / Children's Book / Graphic Novel /
Poetry Collection / Non-Fiction / Memoir / Essay Collection /
Literary Journal / Cookbook / Other
Why: complete coverage of all formats a writer might use.

Genre: three fields — primary + two sub-genres.
All three feed AI context. Not a dropdown. Not a tag cloud.
Why: richer AI context, more nuanced than single dropdown.

Signup: Email + password → Email verification →
Platform code → Platform access.
Why: security and spam prevention from day one.

Data: InkySwot never sells user data. Ever.
GDPR compliant. Deletion within 30 days.
Why: non-negotiable ethical position.

Pricing philosophy: one tier only. No feature gates.
Why: two tiers create two classes of writer. Rejected on principle.
Orwell — "all animals are equal." InkySwot refuses that logic.

Single tier philosophy: the annual subscription is not a better
product. It is a loyalty reward. The product is identical.
Why: simplifies everything — marketing, support, development.

Ada: female, named after Ada Lovelace, voice fixed as
Google UK English Female. Sara Martin will NOT voice Ada.
Why: Ada Lovelace is the first programmer. Appropriate and meaningful.

Cover thumbnail on Overview — REMOVED. Not being built.
AI-generated cover concepts — post-launch only. Not being built now.
Why: scope control. Focus on core platform first.

AUTHENTICATION
Email + password only. No SSO. No third party authentication.
Why: keeps security model clean. No third party dependency.
Consistent with email verification layer.
Locked: 25 May 2026.
