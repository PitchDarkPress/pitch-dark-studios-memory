# Pitch Dark Press — Room
*Last updated: 19 May 2026*

---

## CONCEPT

The Pitch Dark Press room. Vast dark architectural space —
same engine as all Pitch Dark Studios rooms. Built on
The Room architecture (Three.js, vanilla JavaScript,
single HTML file, GitHub Pages deployment).

This room exists permanently — not just as a coming soon
page but as part of Pitch Dark Press's identity. Forever.

---

## HERO OBJECT

A Victorian standing desk. Centre of the space.
Under a single bare bulb hanging from above.
On the desk — printing press letter blocks.
Individual metal type. The raw material of publishing.
Beside them — a printing chase. Set. Ready.
The words already chosen. Waiting to be pressed.

---

## ATMOSPHERE

In the corner — a simple printing press.
Just visible in the shadows beyond the desk light.
Dark. Heavy. Patient.
It has printed things before.
It will print things again.

---

## SOUND

The rhythmic thud and hiss of a printing press running.
In the background. Always there.
Felt more than heard.
The room is never quite silent.

---

## VISUAL LANGUAGE

Vast dark space. Single bare bulb over the desk.
Warm yellow light — not gold, not amber. Working light.
The letter blocks catch the light — small metal glints
in the darkness. The chase holds them together.
The press in the corner: almost lost in shadow.
Almost. But present.
Deep shadows. Industrial. Cinematic.
Minimal interface. No clutter. Presence over mechanics.

---

## TECHNICAL

Single upload-ready index.html.
Vanilla JavaScript only. Three.js rendering.
GitHub Pages deployment.
No frameworks. No build system.
Built on The Room base architecture.

---

## PRINCIPLES (inherited from The Room bible)

1. No flashy game mechanics
2. No HUD clutter
3. No exposition
4. No over-animation
5. Silence matters — sound is ambient, never demanding
6. Objects imply stories without explaining them
7. Atmosphere takes priority over realism
8. The room must feel inhabited instead of generated
9. The room must feel older than the technology creating it
10. Presence over mechanics
