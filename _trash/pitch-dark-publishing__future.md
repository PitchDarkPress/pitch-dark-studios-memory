## PLATFORM EVOLUTION

### Print on Demand Integration
Users design in PD Publishing. Order physical copies
through a print on demand partner. No stock. No risk.
The reader orders. The book/magazine/newspaper prints.

### Distribution Network
Digital distribution to major platforms —
Amazon / Apple Books / Google Play / Kobo and others.
One click from PD Publishing to global distribution.

### PD Art Deep Integration
Design visuals in Pitch Dark Art.
Publish them in Pitch Dark Publishing.
The two platforms talk directly to each other.
A writer illustrates their own picture book without
leaving the Pitch Dark Studios ecosystem.

### InkySwot Integration
Write it in InkySwot. Publish it in PD Publishing.
The full creative pipeline within one ecosystem:
Idea → World building → Writing → Publishing.

### Rights Marketplace
Writers and creators sell their publications
or license their content to others.
Connects to the Rights Marketplace concept
identified in InkySwot future.md.

### Education Tier
Schools and universities create their own
newspapers, magazines and books.
Student journalism. School yearbooks.
Creative writing anthologies.
Separate education pricing tier.

### Community Features
- Publication rating and discovery
- Follow favourite publishers
- Share and recommend publications
- Creator profiles and portfolios
