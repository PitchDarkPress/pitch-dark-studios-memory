## LONGER TERM

These ideas were identified during development.
None are immediate. All are real.

### Terminal Evolution
The Shakespeare fragments could expand over time.
New literary fragments added periodically.
The terminal as a living archive.
Never explained. Never announced.

### Hidden Archive Areas
Spaces that exist but cannot be reached.
Glimpsed in the darkness beyond the desk light.
Implies a larger world without showing it.

### Corridor Systems
Passages leading somewhere unknown.
The room connects to other rooms.
But the other rooms are never seen.
Only implied.

### Connected Rooms
The division rooms eventually connect.
Moving between them becomes possible.
The Pitch Dark Studios ecosystem as
a navigable space.
A long way off. Worth keeping.

### Portal Transitions from Studios Hub
The flower petals on pitchdarkstudios.com
become actual portals into each division room.
Click a petal — enter a room.
The hub and the rooms become one experience.

### Symbolic Objects Per Division
Each room eventually gets a hidden object
that connects it back to the hub.
Something small. Something meaningful.
Never explained.

### Ambient Sound Evolution
The sound layer grows over time.
Seasonal variations.
Time-of-day variations.
Events that trigger subtle sound changes.
Always felt more than heard.

### The Room as Persistent Universe
The long game.
All rooms connected.
The entire Pitch Dark Studios ecosystem
as a single explorable dark space.
Each division a room in the same building.
The building has no end.
