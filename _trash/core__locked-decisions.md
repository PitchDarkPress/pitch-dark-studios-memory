## THE ROOMS

Every division website that is coming soon has a Three.js
room as its landing page. Built on the architecture of
The Room (pitchdarkpress.github.io/pitch-dark-studio-the-room/).
Each room is unique to its division — same engine, different
objects, different atmosphere.

The space exists outside of logic. No walls. No ceiling.
No explanation. Just darkness and what lives in it.

PITCH DARK STUDIOS
Hero: Heavy wooden desk, old style black computer terminal
(green phosphor glow, cursor blinking). No window.
Light from above only. The room is sealed.
Sound: Calm tone — minimal, atmospheric. Between music
and silence. The sound of concentration.

PITCH DARK ART
Hero: Easel with the Mona Lisa. Single light from above.
Corner: Window looking out onto a garden. Night.
Moonlight stabbing through clouds. Cold silver light.
The garden is dark and mysterious.
Sound: Vivaldi — The Four Seasons.

PITCH DARK BROADCASTING
Hero: 1940s ribbon microphone on a stand. Music stand
beside it with the opening page of The Sun Also Rises.
Atmosphere: Red ON AIR light hanging in mid air, floating,
no visible fixture. Just the red glow suspended in darkness.
No window. The room is sealed.
Sound: The speeches of Winston Churchill.

PITCH DARK PUBLISHING
Hero: London newspaper box. Street worn. Dark green.
Gold lettering. Single light from above.
Atmosphere: A gas lamp standing alone in the darkness
at a distance. Warm amber glow. No explanation.
It simply exists there in the dark.
Sound: City ambient sounds.

R&D
Hero: A lathe. Heavy. Industrial. Single light from above.
Atmosphere: A toolbox on the floor a little distance away.
Closed. Worn. The suggestion of work done and work to come.
Sound: Engine sounds. Something is always running in R&D.
