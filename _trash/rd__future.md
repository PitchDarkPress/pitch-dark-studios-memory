## SIMON — FUTURE

### THE TIMELORD
Simon exists within a wider framework called
THE TIMELORD. What this is has not yet been
fully defined. It is forming.
Simon may be one component of something larger.

### Simon — Feature Roadmap
First and only immediate feature:
Timer/reminder notes.
Simon drifts over holding a note.
"Buddy… 12 minutes left."

After that — only add features that serve the
emotional core. Never the other way around.

### Simon — Platform Expansion
Currently Electron — desktop only.
Future possibilities:
- Mobile companion (iOS / Android)
- Browser extension version
- InkySwot integration — Simon lives inside
  InkySwot and knows the writer's project
- Ada and Simon — two companions, different roles.
  Ada knows your world. Simon just lives in it.

### Simon — Commercial Potential
Simon could become a standalone product.
Free tier with basic companion behaviour.
Paid tier with deeper personality and features.
This is a future decision — not yet.
Protect the emotional core first.
