## SIMON — LOCKED DECISIONS

### What Simon Is
A desktop companion. A tiny resident of the machine.
Tagline: little rockets, big feelings.
Primary success metric:
"I miss Simon when he isn't there."
This is more important than any feature.

### Simon — Core Philosophy
Remove complexity until only personality remains.
The more complicated Simon became, the less alive
he felt. This lesson is locked.
Do not add features that bury the emotional core.

### Simon — Movement — LOCKED
Simon does not animate. Simon behaves.
Rocket hover is the definitive locomotion. Final.
Walking legs — abandoned. Will not return.
Tank tracks — abandoned. Will not return.
Floating ball — superseded by rockets.
Simon moves only with intention.
Simon should feel like a presence, not a screensaver.

### Simon — Design — LOCKED
Off-yellow pager/PDA-inspired body.
Rounded square silhouette.
Slightly worn casing. Soft CRT warmth.
Not metallic. Not corporate.
Sleepy half-lidded eyes. Expressive brows.
Simple emotional mouth.
Thin rubber-hose arms.
Tiny retro thrusters — part of his personality.

### Simon — Dialogue — LOCKED
Observational. Dry. Companionable.
Slightly existential. Funny without trying.
Comments on life. Does not issue commands.

### Simon — Night Mode — LOCKED
Nightshirt and cap. Cocoa in screen corner.
Hovers lower. Moves slower. More existential.
This direction continues. It does not change.

### Simon — What NOT To Build
No dashboards.
No productivity suites.
No giant feature systems.
No heavy AI integration.
Protect the emotional core above all else.
First and only immediate feature: timer/reminder notes.

### Simon — Technical Stack — LOCKED
Electron / HTML / CSS / JavaScript.
Transparent always-on-top desktop window.
This does not change for the current phase.
