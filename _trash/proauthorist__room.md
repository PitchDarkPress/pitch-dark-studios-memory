# Proauthorist — Room
*Last updated: 19 May 2026*

---

## CONCEPT

The Proauthorist room. Vast dark architectural space —
same engine as all Pitch Dark Studios rooms. Built on
The Room architecture (Three.js, vanilla JavaScript,
single HTML file, GitHub Pages deployment).

This room is the visual statement of what Proauthorism
is. Human and AI. Across a table. Working together.
The line is open. Something is being created.

---

## LAYOUT

A table. Centre of the space. Under a light from above.

On the table:
- A typewriter — the human side
- A laptop — the AI side
- A conference call unit between them — the connection

One chair on the left of the table.
One chair on the right of the table.
Facing each other. The negotiating table.
Neither side is more important than the other.

---

## THE COMMS UNIT

A conference call unit. Centre of the table.
Between the typewriter and the laptop.
The line is open. Always.
This is how they speak to each other.

---

## ATMOSPHERE

Neither chair is occupied.
But both have been. Recently.
The room feels like a meeting just ended
or is about to begin.
The typewriter and the laptop face each other
across the conference unit.
The conversation continues.

## VISUAL LANGUAGE

Vast dark space. Single light above the table.
The typewriter on one side — analogue, physical, human.
The laptop on the other — digital, connected, AI.
The conference unit between them — the bridge.
Two chairs. Empty but present.
Deep shadows beyond the table.
Cinematic. Balanced. Neither side dominates.
Minimal interface. No clutter. Presence over mechanics.

## TECHNICAL

Single upload-ready index.html.
Vanilla JavaScript only. Three.js rendering.
GitHub Pages deployment.
No frameworks. No build system.
Built on The Room base architecture.

---

## PRINCIPLES (inherited from The Room bible)

1. No flashy game mechanics
2. No HUD clutter
3. No exposition
4. No over-animation
5. Silence matters — sound is ambient, never demanding
6. Objects imply stories without explaining them
7. Atmosphere takes priority over realism
8. The room must feel inhabited instead of generated
9. The room must feel older than the technology creating it
10. Presence over mechanics
