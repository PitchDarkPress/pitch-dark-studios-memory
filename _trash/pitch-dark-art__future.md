# Pitch Dark Art — Future
*Last updated: 19 May 2026*

---

## NOTE

Pitch Dark Art is itself a future project — it is entirely
in the planning phase. This file holds ideas that go beyond
the initial concept, for when the platform is established.

---

## INTEGRATION IDEAS

### InkySwot Integration
AI-generated cover concepts from within InkySwot.
Writer builds their world, Pitch Dark Art generates
visual concepts based on the project data.
Mood pieces, not finished covers.
Flagged as a future idea in InkySwot future.md.

### The Rights Marketplace
Writers sell commissioned art alongside their writing.
Connects to the Rights Marketplace concept in InkySwot.

---

## PLATFORM IDEAS

### Style Profiles
Named visual style profiles — the art equivalent of
InkySwot's AI Voice Profiles. User builds and saves
their own visual identity over time.

### Collaboration with Pitch Dark Broadcasting
Visualisation of audio content — album art, podcast
covers, broadcast graphics.

### Education Tier
Art tools for schools and universities.
Mirrors the InkySwot for Education idea.
