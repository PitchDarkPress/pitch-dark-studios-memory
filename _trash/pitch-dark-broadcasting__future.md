## RECORDING STUDIO — pitchdarkbroadcasting.com/recording

A full online recording studio within the Pitch Dark
Broadcasting family. Standalone product.

What it enables:
- Music recording
- Radio play production
- Audiobook recording and production
- TV programme production
- Full suite of recording facilities online

Latency solution — LOCKED 19 May 2026:
No real-time transmission required. Users record locally
on any device — phone / tablet / PC / Mac — using a
simple recording facility provided by the platform.
Raw material is then uploaded to the platform for
online editing and production.
The platform is the editing and publishing suite.
The device is the recording studio.
Barrier to entry: almost zero. Everyone already has
a recording device in their pocket.

Open decisions:
- Technology stack
- Integration with Radio platform
- Integration with InkySwot (write it, record it)
- Integration with Ada audiobook production (future)
- Pricing model
- MVP definition
