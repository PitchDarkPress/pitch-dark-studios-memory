# Pitch Dark Publishing — Room
*Last updated: 18 May 2026*

---

## CONCEPT

The Publishing room. Vast dark architectural space — same
engine as all Pitch Dark Studios rooms. Built on The Room
architecture (Three.js, vanilla JavaScript, single HTML file,
GitHub Pages deployment).

The space exists outside of logic. No walls. No ceiling.
No explanation. Just darkness and what lives in it.

---

## HERO OBJECT

A London newspaper box. Centre of the space.
Under a single light from above.
Street worn. Dark green. Gold lettering.
The physical, printed, distributed word.

---

## ATMOSPHERE

In the distance — a gas lamp. Standing alone in the darkness.
Warm amber glow. Victorian. The light bleeds into the black.
No explanation of why it is there. It simply is.

---

## SOUND

City ambient sounds.
The hum of a city. Traffic distant. People passing.
Life happening somewhere out there in the dark.

---

## VISUAL LANGUAGE

Vast dark space. Single pool of light on the newspaper box.
The gas lamp in the distance — warm amber bleeding into black.
Two light sources. Two pools of warmth in the darkness.
Deep shadows between them. Cinematic darkness.
Minimal interface. No clutter. Presence over mechanics.

---

## TECHNICAL

Single upload-ready index.html.
Vanilla JavaScript only. Three.js rendering.
GitHub Pages deployment.
No frameworks. No build system.
Built on The Room base architecture.

---

## PRINCIPLES (inherited from The Room bible)

1. No flashy game mechanics
2. No HUD clutter
3. No exposition
4. No over-animation
5. Silence matters — sound is ambient, never demanding
6. Objects imply stories without explaining them
7. Atmosphere takes priority over realism
8. The room must feel inhabited instead of generated
9. The room must feel older than the technology creating it
10. Presence over mechanics
