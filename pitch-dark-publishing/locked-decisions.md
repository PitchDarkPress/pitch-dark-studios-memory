---

## IDENTITY

Name: Pitch Dark Publishing.
A Pitch Dark Studios division.
A digital publishing platform — not a publishing imprint.
Pitch Dark Press is the imprint. Pitch Dark Publishing
is the platform that enables others to do what
Pitch Dark Press does.

---

## PHILOSOPHY — NON-NEGOTIABLE

Egalitarian to its core.
Anyone can create. From hobby to fully professional.
No gatekeepers. No prior experience required.
Proauthorist in its DNA — declared always.
This is not a tool for professionals only.
This is a tool for everyone.

---

## CORE PRODUCTS

Four main elements. Everything else is added later.
Build order to be decided — see open decisions.

1. NEWSPAPERS
2. MAGAZINES
3. COMIC BOOKS
4. PICTURE BOOKS

Additional products are candy floss — added over time
to keep the platform growing. Never before the core
four are established and working.

---

## THE PLATFORM MODEL

Template-based — the Wix model for publishing.
Professional quality templates from day one.
On/off model — users activate only what they need.
A newspaper creator does not see comic book tools.
Clean. Focused. Never overwhelming.

## INTEGRATION

Pitch Dark Art: design visuals in PD Art, publish
in PD Publishing. These two platforms will link.
InkySwot: write in InkySwot, publish in PD Publishing.
The full Pitch Dark Studios creative pipeline.
