# The Room — Locked Decisions
*Last updated: 19 May 2026*

These decisions are final. Do not revisit unless Kev
explicitly asks, or unless it seems wise to flag something.

---

## IDENTITY

The Room is the interior of Pitch Dark Studios.
It is a joke. The most exclusive room in the world.

It is also the base architecture for every division
coming soon room across the entire ecosystem.
Build it once. Skin it for each division.

And it is this:
"One person building impossible systems alone
in the dark."

That truth is locked. It does not change.

---

## THE CORE CONCEPT — LOCKED

A solitary desk in darkness.
No visible walls. Black floor. Vast void.
The darkness defines the architecture.
Symbolic space. Psychological architecture.
Implied reality. Abstracted creative bunker.

The large library/archive concept was abandoned.
It will not return.

---

## THE PHILOSOPHY — LOCKED

The room must feel like someone intelligent
occupies it. Work is actively underway.
The owner stepped out moments ago.
The visitor should not be there.

The room NEVER explains itself.
Interpretation is intentionally impossible
to fully resolve. That ambiguity is the point.

IMPLICATION over EXPLANATION. Always.
Nothing over-performs. No gimmicks.
No obvious lore delivery. No exposition.

---

## LOCKED CANON ELEMENTS

All of these are confirmed and permanent:
- Vast black void. No visible walls.
- Black floor. Single isolated desk.
- Old CRT-style computer console.
- Green terminal text. Slow blinking cursor.
- Shakespeare fragments cycling slowly.
- One institutional black metal chair.
- Cramped notebook beside terminal.
- Small set of keys beside notebook.
- Small rug beneath desk.
- Red system LED on computer.
- Cigarette resting on ashtray rim.
- Cigarette ember glowing softly.
- Slow smoke curl.
- Soft white overhead light.
- User begins at distance from desk.
- Ability to walk fully around desk.
- Fast smooth movement.
- Human-scale camera height.

---

## THE CIGARETTE — LOCKED

The cigarette stays. Always.
It implies strain, obsession, sleeplessness,
interruption, immediate recent presence.
"Someone was just here."
A cup of tea was rejected — too warm, too friendly.
The cigarette is correct. It does not change.

---

## THE TERMINAL — LOCKED

Archive residue. Institutional system output.
Forgotten literary memory.
Shakespeare fragments and archive language.
The blinking cursor is proof of life.
It does not become flashy. Ever.

---

## SOUND PHILOSOPHY — LOCKED

Infrastructural. Environmental. Accidental.
Mechanical. Silence dominating everything.
The room changes how the user feels without
them understanding why.
No soundtrack. No obvious ambient loops.
No theatrical sounds.

---

## TECHNICAL — LOCKED

Three.js. Single HTML file.
Vanilla JavaScript only. GitHub Pages.
No frameworks. No build system.
Lightweight. Atmospheric minimalism.
Illusion over geometry.
Darkness replacing architecture.
This does not change.
