# The Room — Bible

## Identity
- Hidden creative command centre
- Obsessive writer's war room
- Private intelligence bunker
- Workshop of a brilliant lunatic
- Architectural mind of Pitch Dark Studios itself
- "The most exclusive room in the world"

## Creative Philosophy — Locked Rules
1. No flashy game mechanics
2. No HUD clutter
3. No exposition
4. No over-animation
5. Silence matters
6. Objects imply stories without explaining them
7. Atmosphere takes priority over realism
8. The room must feel inhabited instead of generated
9. The room must feel older than the technology creating it
10. Presence over mechanics

## Visual Language
- Vast dark architectural space
- Towering shelves
- Warm amber light pools
- Deep shadows between structures
- Rain-streaked industrial window
- Investigation/archive walls
- Heavy wooden desk at centre
- Typewriter silhouette
- Dust-filled air
- Cinematic darkness
- Minimal interface
- Architectural loneliness

## Technical Direction
- Single upload-ready index.html files
- Vanilla JavaScript only
- Three.js rendering
- GitHub Pages deployment
- No frameworks, no build system
- Upload-ready workflow only

## Final Principle
The room succeeds when it feels like a place somebody could disappear into for years.
