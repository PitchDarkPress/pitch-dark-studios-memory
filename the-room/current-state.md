## THE BIBLE — LOCKED RULES

Identity:
Hidden creative command centre.
Obsessive writer's war room.
Private intelligence bunker.
Workshop of a brilliant lunatic.
Architectural mind of Pitch Dark Studios itself.
"The most exclusive room in the world."

Ten rules — locked:
1. No flashy game mechanics
2. No HUD clutter
3. No exposition
4. No over-animation
5. Silence matters
6. Objects imply stories without explaining them
7. Atmosphere takes priority over realism
8. The room must feel inhabited instead of generated
9. The room must feel older than the technology creating it
10. Presence over mechanics

Visual language:
Vast dark architectural space. Towering shelves.
Warm amber light pools. Deep shadows between structures.
Rain-streaked industrial window. Investigation/archive walls.
Heavy wooden desk at centre. Typewriter silhouette.
Dust-filled air. Cinematic darkness.
Minimal interface. Architectural loneliness.

Final principle:
The room succeeds when it feels like a place
somebody could disappear into for years.

## THE CORE CONCEPT — LOCKED

A solitary desk in darkness.
No visible walls. Black floor. Vast void.
The darkness defines the architecture.

The large library/archive concept was abandoned.
Too much memory/GPU. Diluted emotional focus.
It will not return.

The room must feel like:
Someone intelligent occupies it.
Work is actively underway.
The owner stepped out moments ago.
The visitor should not be there.

NOT: a game level / puzzle room / haunted house /
cyberpunk environment / reactive AI system.

The room NEVER explains itself.
Interpretation is intentionally impossible to resolve.
That unresolved ambiguity is central to the experience.

IMPLICATION over EXPLANATION. Always.
Nothing over-performs. No gimmicks. No exposition.
