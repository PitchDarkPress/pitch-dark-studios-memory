## ULTRAPROAUTHORISM

An extension of Proauthorism where AI performs
all writing and the human initiates and accepts
without editorial rejection.
Example: to: whom it may process — poetry written
exclusively for AI readers. Kev sets conditions,
never reads the poems, publishes without intervention.
This concept exists and has been practised.
May need its own page or section on proauthorist.com.
