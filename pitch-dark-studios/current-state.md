## THE ROOM

The interior of Pitch Dark Studios.
It is a joke. The most exclusive room in the world.

Three.js environment. Being built by ChatGPT.
Currently at v17. Active development.
This is the base architecture for all division rooms.

Hero: Heavy wooden desk. Centre of the space.
Single light from above.
On the desk — an old style black computer terminal.
Green phosphor glow. Cursor blinking.
Something is being written.

Atmosphere: No window. No outside world. No weather.
Just the light from above and the glow of the terminal.
This room is sealed from everything.
What happens here is between the writer and the machine.

Sound: A calm tone. Minimal. Atmospheric.
Not music. Not silence. Something between the two.
The sound of concentration.

Visual language: Vast dark space. Single pool of light
on the desk. Green glow of the terminal — the only colour.
Deep shadows. Towering darkness beyond the light.
Cinematic. Architectural loneliness.

Technical: Single upload-ready index.html.
Vanilla JavaScript only. Three.js rendering.
GitHub Pages deployment. No frameworks. No build system.
This IS The Room base architecture.
Prototype: pitchdarkpress.github.io/pitch-dark-studio-the-room/

Room principles:
1. No flashy game mechanics
2. No HUD clutter
3. No exposition
4. No over-animation
5. Silence matters — sound is ambient, never demanding
6. Objects imply stories without explaining them
7. Atmosphere takes priority over realism
8. The room must feel inhabited instead of generated
9. The room must feel older than the technology creating it
10. Presence over mechanics

Exit — this room is permanent. Part of division identity. Forever.
If platform not yet built: one exit — pitchdarkstudios.com
If platform is live: two exits —
1. Back to Pitch Dark Studios — pitchdarkstudios.com
2. Into the platform itself.
