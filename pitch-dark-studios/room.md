# Pitch Dark Studios — Room
*Last updated: 18 May 2026*

---

## CONCEPT

The hub room. The interior of Pitch Dark Studios itself.
Vast dark architectural space — the base architecture
from which all other rooms flow.
Built on The Room (Three.js, vanilla JavaScript, single
HTML file, GitHub Pages deployment).

The joke: Pitch Dark Studios is a room.
The most exclusive room in the world.

---

## HERO OBJECT

A heavy wooden desk. Centre of the space.
Under a single light from above.
On the desk — an old style black computer terminal.
The kind that predates everything. Green phosphor glow.
The cursor blinks. Something is being written.

---

## ATMOSPHERE

No window. No outside world. No weather.
Just the light from above and the glow of the terminal.
This room is sealed from everything.
What happens here is between the writer and the machine.

---

## SOUND

A calm tone. Minimal. Atmospheric.
Not music. Not silence. Something between the two.
The sound of concentration.

---

## VISUAL LANGUAGE

Vast dark space. Single pool of light on the desk.
The green glow of the terminal screen — the only colour.
Deep shadows. Towering darkness beyond the light.
Cinematic. Architectural loneliness.
Minimal interface. No clutter. Presence over mechanics.

---

## TECHNICAL

Single upload-ready index.html.
Vanilla JavaScript only. Three.js rendering.
GitHub Pages deployment.
No frameworks. No build system.
This IS The Room base architecture.

The Room (pitchdarkpress.github.io/pitch-dark-studio-the-room/)
is the working prototype. This is the final version.

---

## PRINCIPLES (from The Room bible)

1. No flashy game mechanics
2. No HUD clutter
3. No exposition
4. No over-animation
5. Silence matters — sound is ambient, never demanding
6. Objects imply stories without explaining them
7. Atmosphere takes priority over realism
8. The room must feel inhabited instead of generated
9. The room must feel older than the technology creating it
10. Presence over mechanics
